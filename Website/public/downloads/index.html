Syndicate Loader for Mac

A Mac companion that helps you get an IPA onto your iPhone or iPad. Loader is the installer tool—not the messaging app. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac.

Download Loader 0.1.4
  1. Download the iPhone / iPad IPA and install Loader on your Mac.
  2. Connect your iPhone or iPad over USB, trust the Mac, and select the IPA in Loader.
  3. Use Loader’s signing step with your Apple signing credential and matching device profiles, then install. Enable Developer Mode if prompted.

Developer ID signed, Apple notarized, and local-first. Installs compatible IPAs over USB or trusted network connections and detects provisioning problems before installation.

Start with USB. Trusted Wi-Fi installation is also available when your paired device is discoverable.
